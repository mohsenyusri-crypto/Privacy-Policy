Privacy Notice

This notice explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

1. What Information Do We Collect?

Personal Information You Provide

In brief: We collect personal information that you voluntarily disclose.

We collect personal information you provide when you register for our services, express interest in our organization or offerings, participate in service activities, or otherwise communicate with us.

Personal Information Provided by You. The personal information we collect varies according to the nature of your interactions with us and our Services, your preferences, and the products or features you utilize. Such information may include:

  • Names
  • Telephone numbers
  • Email addresses
  • Passwords

Sensitive Information. We do not process sensitive data.

Application Data. When you use our application(s), we may collect additional information if you grant the necessary access or permissions:

Push Notifications. We may request permission to send push notifications concerning your account or specific application features. You may disable these communications via your device settings.

This information is used primarily to ensure the security and functionality of our application(s), to support troubleshooting, and for internal analytics and reporting.

All personal information you provide must be accurate, complete, and current; you are responsible for notifying us of any modifications.

2. How Do We Process Your Information?

In Brief: We process your information to deliver, enhance, and administer our Services; to communicate with you; to maintain security and prevent fraud; and to comply with legal obligations. We may also process your information for additional purposes with your consent.

We process your personal information for various reasons based on your interactions with our Services, including:

  • Account Creation and Authentication. We may process your information to enable account registration, login, and ongoing account management.
  • Service Delivery. We may process your information to provide the requested services.
  • Security. We may process your information to protect our systems and users.

3. When and With Whom Do We Share Your Personal Information?

Summary: We may disclose information in the circumstances outlined below and/or to the specified third parties.

We may disclose your personal information in the following circumstances:

  • Business Transfers. We may share or transfer your information in connection with, or during negotiations of, any merger, sale of company assets, financing, or acquisition of all or part of our business by another entity.
  • Business Partners. We may disclose your information to our business partners to provide products, services, or promotions.

4. Do We Use Cookies and Other Tracking Technologies?

Summary: We may employ cookies and similar technologies to collect and store your information.

We may utilize cookies and comparable tracking technologies (e.g., web beacons and pixels) to collect data during your interaction with our Services. Certain tracking technologies support the security of our Services and your account, prevent system failures, resolve technical issues, preserve your preferences, and facilitate core site functions.

We also authorize third parties and service providers to deploy tracking technologies on our Services for analytics and advertising purposes, including managing and displaying advertisements or customizing content to your interests. These entities may use their technologies to deliver interest-based advertising for products and services, which may appear on our Services or other websites.

Detailed information regarding our use of these technologies and options for declining certain cookies is provided in our Cookie Notice.

5. How Long Do We Retain Your Information?

Summary: We retain your information for as long as necessary to achieve the purposes described in this Privacy Notice, unless otherwise required by law.

We retain personal information only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Notice, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law (e.g., tax, accounting, or other legal obligations). No purpose outlined herein necessitates retention beyond the duration of a user’s account.

When a legitimate business need no longer exists, we will delete or anonymize the information. If this is not feasible—such as when data reside in backup archives—we will securely store and isolate the information from further processing until deletion can occur.

6. How Do We Secure Your Information?

In Short: We employ organizational and technical security measures to protect your personal information.

We have implemented appropriate and reasonable technical and organizational safeguards intended to protect the security of all personal information we process. Nevertheless, no electronic transmission over the Internet or storage technology can be guaranteed to be entirely secure; accordingly, we cannot ensure that unauthorized parties, including hackers or cybercriminals, will not circumvent our controls to collect, access, misappropriate, or alter your information. Although we endeavor to protect your data, transmission to and from our Services is at your own risk. We recommend accessing the Services only within a secure environment.

7. What Are Your Privacy Rights?

In Short: Depending on your country, province, or state of residence, you may review, modify, or terminate your account at any time.

Withdrawal of Consent

Where we rely on your consent—whether express or implied, as defined by applicable law—to process your personal information, you may withdraw such consent at any time. To do so, please contact us using the information provided in the “How Can You Contact Us About This Notice?” section below.

Please be advised that withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing conducted prior to the withdrawal. Furthermore, where permitted by law, it does not affect processing performed on alternative lawful bases.

Account Information

You may review, modify, or terminate your account at any time by:

  • Accessing your account settings to update your information; or
  • Requesting termination, which will result in deactivation or deletion of your account and associated data from our active databases.

We may retain certain information to prevent fraud, resolve technical issues, support investigations, enforce our terms, or comply with legal obligations.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

Most web browsers are configured to accept cookies by default. You may typically configure your browser to delete or reject cookies; however, doing so may impair certain features or services.

For inquiries regarding your privacy rights, please contact us at mohsenyusri@albakri.technology.

Controls for Do-Not-Track Features

Most web browsers, certain mobile operating systems, and mobile applications offer a Do-Not-Track (“DNT”) feature that allows users to express a preference against the monitoring and collection of their online activity. At present, no uniform technical standard for recognizing or implementing DNT signals has been established. Consequently, we do not currently respond to DNT signals or other automated mechanisms indicating a preference not to be tracked. Should a binding standard for online tracking be adopted in the future, we will disclose our practices in a revised version of this Privacy Notice.
